# Proof in Motion

Why does the world work the way it does?

Proof in Motion follows the clues behind Earth's strangest landscapes, boldest feats of engineering and most surprising stories from history. Through animated explainers, we connect the evidence to the bigger picture and make complex ideas easy to see.

[Visit the channel on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@proofinmotiontv).

## Proof in Motion Studio

Proof in Motion Studio is the channel owners' private production and publishing application. It uses YouTube API Services to upload owner-approved videos and retrieve the owners' channel analytics. It is an internal command-line application, without public accounts or a hosted login page. These documents are publicly accessible; the application and channel credentials are private.
